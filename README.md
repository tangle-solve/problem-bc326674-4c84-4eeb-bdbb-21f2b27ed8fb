### ALGO-000152: Dürer’s edge-unfolding conjecture
- **Subfield:** Computational geometry
- **Open:** Prove that every convex polyhedron can be cut along edges into one nonoverlapping planar net, or give a counterexample.
- **Status:** Rooted in Dürer’s 1525 work; the modern universal conjecture remains open despite many special classes.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Connects polyhedral geometry to fabrication and foldable structures.
- **Key works:** Dürer (1525), *Underweysung der Messung*; Shephard (1975), *Convex Polytopes with Convex Nets*; TOPP Problem 9.
- **Related:** Manufacturing—single-sheet polyhedral fabrication.
